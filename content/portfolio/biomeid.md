      ### Sovereign Digital Identity for Nations & Confederations

      **BiomeID** is an advanced digital identity platform engineered for countries and distributed federations to build and administer their own sovereign national digital identity infrastructure using a proprietary, high-assurance blockchain.

      #### Technical Architecture
      * **Proprietary Sovereign Blockchain**: High-throughput distributed ledger providing deterministic finality and cryptographic consensus free from foreign cloud dependency.
      * **Zero-Knowledge Privacy Proofs**: Enabling citizens to prove identity, age, legal credentials, and entitlement without disclosing underlying private records.
      * **National Scale Interoperability**: Seamlessly integrating with passport gates, financial KYC, voting verification, and municipal public service platforms.
