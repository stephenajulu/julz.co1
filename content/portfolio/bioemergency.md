      ### Vital Biological Markers When Seconds Matter

      **BioEmergency** is a personal digital health service providing critical emergency information storage. In acute situations where patients may be unresponsive, BioEmergency enables accredited first responders to rapidly access vital markers and medical data to save lives.

      #### Security & Functionality
      * **Emergency Biological Markers**: Blood type, severe allergies, critical health conditions, and emergency contact details stored securely.
      * **Instant Responders Access**: NFC and encrypted QR access for verified emergency personnel with zero-knowledge cryptographic safeguards.
      * **Continuous Availability**: Sovereign data architecture ensuring emergency medical records are accessible even during localized hospital network downtime.
