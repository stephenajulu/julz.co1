      ### Sovereign Infrastructure in Silicon & Software

      **TausiNode** manages open-source business infrastructure with the power of both software and custom preloaded hardware. By pairing the complete Managed Open Source Business Infrastructure (MOSBI) stack with plug-and-play Mini PCs, TausiNode delivers turnkey data sovereignty for organizations and homelab users.

      #### Preloaded Hardware & Software Stack
      * **Turnkey Mini PC Hardware**: Pre-configured, power-efficient industrial Mini PCs engineered for silent, 24/7 continuous operation.
      * **Authentik**: Unified Single Sign-On (SSO) and identity management gateway.
      * **Nextcloud Hub**: Sovereign cloud storage, file synchronization, and team collaboration.
      * **ERPNext**: Full-suite ERP covering finance, supply chain, CRM, and human resources.
      * **n8n**: Distributed workflow automation and webhook orchestration.
      * **NetBird**: WireGuard-powered zero-trust peer-to-peer overlay network.
      * **RustDesk & OneUptime**: Remote server management, status monitoring, and uptime observability.
      * **Homelab Edition**: Dedicated turnkey setup for privacy-focused individuals and researchers running independent local nodes.
