      ### Urban Electric Micro-Mobility for Nairobi

      **Nairobyke** is an innovative micro-mobility and hardware rental service modeled on urban bike-share concepts like Citibike, purpose-engineered for the specific urban landscape, road realities, and commuting patterns of Nairobi.

      #### Hardware & System Features
      * **Durable Urban e-Bikes**: Ruggedized electric bicycles engineered for rough terrain, high load, and daily commuting.
      * **Solar Swapping Stations**: Distributed solar-powered battery swapping hubs strategically placed along key Nairobi transit routes.
      * **Connected Fleet Telemetry**: Real-time GPS tracking, anti-theft immobilization, and mobile commuter application.
